## 0.1.0 (2026-11-20)

- First version
- Records text (with formatting), images and files; copying the same thing again moves it to the top
- History window on ⌃⌥V: search, choose with ↑↓ or ⌘1–9, paste right away, hold ⌥ for plain text
- Pin, delete, and undo delete with ⌘Z
- Skips copies password managers mark as concealed and copies from excluded apps; pause recording
- 10 most recent items in the menu bar menu
- Getting Started, Settings (General, Excluded Apps, Shortcuts, About), custom shortcut
- In-app updates, Korean and English
